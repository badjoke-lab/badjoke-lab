Operating principles

What the projects try to preserve

Source traceability

Material claims should lead back to identifiable sources rather than anonymous summaries.

History before snapshots

Current state matters, but so do migrations, closures, incidents, rebrands, restrictions, and unresolved transitions.

Explicit uncertainty

Unknown, incomplete, disputed, and not-yet-reviewed information should remain visible instead of being silently filled in.

No implied recommendation

Registries and dashboards describe recorded conditions. They are not investment, safety, or legal recommendations.
